How to Play

Every game works on a laptop, and almost all of them work on a phone or tablet too.

1Getting started

1

Pick a game

Open the Arcade and press Play on any game, or try Surprise me!

2

Click the game once

Click anywhere inside the game so it can hear your keyboard. Then use the keys below.

3

Come back

Press the browser's Back button (or ⌘ + [ on a Mac, Alt + ← on Windows).

1

Pick a game

Tap Play on any game. Games marked Laptop only need a keyboard, so skip those on a phone.

2

Turn it sideways

Action games are easier with the phone held sideways. The on-screen buttons show up by themselves.

3

Come back

Tap your browser's Back arrow, or swipe in from the left edge of the screen.

5Something not working?

There's no sound
  • Click or tap the game once. Browsers keep games quiet until you touch them.
  • Look for a 🔊 or Sounds button in the corner of the game and make sure it's on.
  • On an iPhone, flip the silent switch on the side (or the Action button) so it's not on silent.
  • Turn up the volume on your laptop or phone.
The keys don't do anything
  • Click inside the game first. If you clicked the address bar or a text box, the game can't hear the keys.
  • Some games only start moving after you press their Start or Play button.
  • Check the controls for that game in the list above. Some use WASD, some use the arrows.
The game is stuck on "loading"
  • Games marked Needs internet (the 3D ones) download their 3D engine when they start, so check your Wi-Fi.
  • Refresh the page: ⌘ + R on a Mac, Ctrl + R on Windows, or pull down on a phone.
  • 3D games are the hardest work for a phone. If one is very slow, try it on a laptop.
I can't see the buttons on my phone
  • Turn your phone sideways, or scroll down a little. Some games put the pad under the game.
  • In a few games the buttons only appear once the level starts, or after you touch the screen.
  • If the game says Laptop only, it doesn't have phone buttons.
The game is too big or too small
  • On a laptop, zoom with ⌘ + / ⌘ − (Mac) or Ctrl + / Ctrl − (Windows). ⌘ 0 or Ctrl 0 resets it.
  • On a phone, turn it sideways and try again.
Found a bug, or have an idea?
  • We'd love to hear it! Send us feedback. Tell us which game, what happened and what you were doing.
  • You don't need to give your name. A nickname is enough.
I can't see a new game
  • Your browser may be showing an old copy. Do a hard refresh: ⌘ + Shift + R on a Mac or Ctrl + Shift + R on Windows.
