Furthest survived: no nights yet

The watch runs midnight to 6 AM. Three campers went missing from this site last spring — Wesley, Birdie, and Otto. The search parties never found them. Something in these woods still wears their shapes to get close to the cabin.

  • Trail cams — cycle through the campground to see what's moving. Watching a trail slows down whatever's on it, but you're blind to your own door while the monitor's up.
  • Cabin door / storm shutter — seal a trail off. Costs power the whole time it's shut, and a shut door doesn't open itself.
  • Flashlight — check what's really standing outside before you open back up. Shining it on whatever's already at an open door is the last thing you'll see.
  • Cellar hatch light — the only thing that sends whatever's down there back underground. Timing matters.
  • Power — hits zero and both the door and the shutter swing open on their own.

Ninety-nine nights. Nobody's stayed that long and still remembered their own name.
